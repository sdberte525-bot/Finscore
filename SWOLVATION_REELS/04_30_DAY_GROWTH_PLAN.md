# 4. 30-day organic growth plan: from faith audience to first customers

**Goals:**
- first 1,000 relevant followers;
- real shares and saves;
- profile visits;
- website clicks;
- first purchases.

**These are targets, not guarantees.** A brand-new account should expect a few hundred views per post at first. Buffer's analysis of 11.4M TikTok posts found a median of about 500 views per post (see `SWOLVATION_FIRST_SALE/02_TREND_RESEARCH.md`). Growth comes from the few posts that break out, which is why we post daily and test hooks.

**Never:** bought followers, bots, engagement pods, follow-for-follow, fake comments, or misleading claims.

## The mix (first 30 days)
- **70% pure Christian content:** quote, scripture and story reels.
- **20% faith + discipline + gym.**
- **10% subtle apparel:** message first, product second, "link in bio" only here.

The 15 launch reels already follow this mix: 10 faith, 3 gym, 2 apparel.

## Daily routine (about 45 minutes)

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Same time daily (pick one slot, e.g. 7 pm local) | Post the day's reel on **Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts**: one MP4, three uploads (see repurposing below) |
| First 60 minutes after posting | Reply to every comment. Pin the best comment, or pin our own comment quoting the full verse. |
| 15 minutes | Leave 10 genuine comments (full sentences) on posts from Christian fitness creators and on #christianfitness / #faith posts |
| 5 minutes | Share the reel to Stories with a poll sticker ("Needed this today? Yes / Amen") |
| Evening | Log views, 3-second hold, average watch time, shares, saves, profile visits and link clicks in the dashboard (`SWOLVATION_FIRST_SALE/08_first_sale_dashboard.xlsx`) |

## Days 16–30: double down on what works
- **Day 16 review.**
  - Rank the 15 reels by **shares per 1,000 views**, then **saves per 1,000**, then **3-second hold**.
  - The top 3 become series.
- **Series slots** (each new episode takes about 20 minutes with the template; `02_VISUAL_AND_AUDIO_IDENTITY.md`):
  - **"Iron Verse" (Mondays):** one KJV verse, narrated, over an engraving, like R02, R08 and R09.
  - **"5 A.M." (Wednesdays):** discipline and gym with faith, like R10–R12.
  - **"The Night Before" (Fridays):** a 20–25s narrated Bible story told in second person, like R03 and R13.
  - **Quote reels (other days):** a new pain-to-hope two-liner, like R01, R05 and R07.
- **Hook re-tests.** Re-post the best two reels on TikTok with hook B (below) 7 days later. On Instagram, test hooks with **Trial Reels**, which go only to non-followers.
- **Apparel:** two more product reels (days 22 and 29), each leading with a verse. Never two product posts in a row.
- **Sunday:** a quiet "rest" post (no CTA), and the weekly review.
- **Week 4:** turn the 3 best quotes into carousels, Instagram's highest-engagement format in 2026 data (research file §1): slide 1 is the quote, slide 2 the verse, slide 3 the end card.

## Repurposing one file across three platforms (no extra editing)

| Platform | Upload | Specifics |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram Reels** | The MP4 + **cover JPG** (`02_COVERS/`) | Use the caption from the reel sheet and 3–5 hashtags (Instagram caps hashtags at 5). Keep "Original audio" and name it `SWOLVATION · <reel name>` so others can reuse the sound and it links back to us. Turn on "Trial" for hook tests. |
| **TikTok** | Same MP4 | **Don't add a trending song on top.** The score is integrated and commercially safe; TikTok business accounts are limited to the Commercial Music Library anyway. Write a shorter caption. Use the cover frame selector, or upload the cover in the editor. |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Same MP4 | Title = the quote or verse (under 60 characters). Description: the verse + "Strength through faith." + the bio link. |
| **Pinterest (optional)** | Cover JPG + link | Faith-quote pins send traffic to the site for a long time |

## Hook variations to test
Each reel sheet (`03_REEL_SHEETS.md`) lists hook A (as rendered) plus hooks B and C. To test:
1. Swap the first card's text in `project/defs/rNN.py`.
2. Re-render with `REUSE=1`. Only the cards and the final composite rebuild, about 3 minutes.
3. Post it as an Instagram Trial Reel, or a TikTok repost 7+ days later.
4. **Judge on 3-second hold rate**, then average watch time. Change one thing at a time.

## What to measure, and what to do about it

| Signal | Where | Healthy early sign | If it's low |
|---|---|---|---|
| First-second / 3-second hold | TikTok Studio retention graph; Instagram "skip rate" | above 60% hold | Test a new first line. Make it shorter and more direct. |
| Average watch time | Both | above 70% of length (replays push it past 100%) | Cut 1–2s. Make the turn land earlier. |
| Completion | TikTok | above 35% on 10–13s reels | Shorten the hold before the end card |
| Shares and sends per 1,000 views | Instagram (sends), TikTok (shares) | above 5 | Write more "send this to…" lines (R07, R09 style) |
| Saves per 1,000 | Both | above 5 | More scripture reels and carousels |
| Profile visits per 1,000 | Both | above 10 | Pin 3 best reels; bio = "Christian gym wear. Scripture on the back." |
| Link clicks | Instagram link taps; TikTok bio link (needs about 1,000 followers or a Business account) | above 2% of profile visits | Add a Story link sticker on the day's reel ("the verse from today's reel is on our tee") |
| Purchases | Shopify "Sessions by referrer" + UTM links | 1 order is the week-2 goal | See the funnel fixes in `SWOLVATION_FIRST_SALE/01_CONVERSION_AUDIT.md` |

**Rule for judging:** wait 48–72 hours and 3 or more posts per format before judging. Small numbers swing wildly.

## From followers to customers (without selling too early)
1. **Bio:** `Christian gym wear. Scripture on the back. ✝` + one UTM link to `/collections/all`.
2. **Every 10th post** is an apparel reel that leads with a verse (R14, R15).
3. **Story bridge:** after any reel whose verse is on a shirt (R02 → the tee, R15 → the long sleeve), post a Story with the product and a link sticker.
4. **Email:** turn on the welcome flow (`SWOLVATION_FIRST_SALE/07_EMAIL_SYSTEM.md`), so followers who click but don't buy still hear about the drop.
5. **Never** comment-bait for sales, buy promotion before reels show real traction, or use urgency we don't have.
