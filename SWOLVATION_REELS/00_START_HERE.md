## How it was made (and what's real)
- **Competitor study:** `01_COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md`.
  - TrustGodBro has **8,638,565 followers and 4,487 posts**, read from Instagram's public embed today.
  - Their visible formula is plain text on black: short "you/I" lines, scripture, and sales kept in a separate account.
  - Their reels and engagement beyond likes and comments **could not be accessed**, so I've said so instead of guessing.
- **Look:**
  - 1860s Gustave Doré Bible engravings (public domain), rain on glass (CC0), and SWOLVATION's own empty-gym AI footage.
  - One new AI image for R13: a man seen only from behind on a rain-soaked road. No faces, no AI "religious figures".
- **Sound:**
  - **Higgsfield has no music or sound-effects model available to us**, so every note was synthesized in our own pipeline.
  - That covers piano, pads, choir, drones, impacts, rain, thunder and the signature end bell.
  - Because nothing is licensed or sampled, the audio is safe on a business account.
- **Narration:**
  - The voice you picked (preset 6705e465…, "Fraser"), generated through Higgsfield's ElevenLabs engine. That engine is the one this voice supports; the default model failed with it.
  - Each line was transcribed back and checked word for word.
- **Scripture:** KJV only (public domain), verified verse by verse, always referenced and always in italic. Original lines are in Cinzel capitals, so scripture and our writing are never confused.
- **QA on every reel:**
  - frame checks at the hook, the turn and the end card;
  - automatic text-overlap detection;
  - loudness and peak measurement;
  - a check that audio and video durations match.
- **Problems found and fixed during QA:**
  - a purple color cast;
  - a dropped end card;
  - lightning that washed out the frame;
  - text collisions;
  - an AI figure that read as a child (retaken);
  - an engraving scanned **upside down** (re-rendered);
  - R11's audio peaking at +0.4 dBTP after encoding (limited to −1.9 dBTP, loudness unchanged).
