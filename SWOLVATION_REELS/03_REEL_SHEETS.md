# 3. The 15 launch reels

Every reel is a finished 1080×1920, 30 fps MP4 with integrated audio, mixed to −14 LUFS. MP4 and cover links are in `00_START_HERE.md`.

**Conventions:**
- **ORIGINAL** = SWOLVATION's own writing (Cinzel caps).
- **KJV** = scripture quoted exactly from the King James Version (public domain), checked against bible-api.com. It's set in italic with its reference.
- **Narration** = your chosen voice, generated through Higgsfield's ElevenLabs engine.
- **Music** = original score from `tools/score.py`. There's no licensed music anywhere.

**Image sources:**
- Doré = Gustave Doré Bible engravings, 1866/1890, public domain.
- Gym/apparel = SWOLVATION's own AI library from earlier campaigns.
- Full list: `05_SOURCES_AND_LICENSES.md`.
