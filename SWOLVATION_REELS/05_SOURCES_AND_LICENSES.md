## Audio
- **Music, ambience and effects:** 100% original, synthesized by `project/tools/score.py`. That includes piano, pads, choir, drones, impacts, risers, bells, rain, wind, thunder and heartbeat.
  - No samples, loops or licensed tracks are used, so the music is safe for a commercial account on every platform.
- **Why music was made in-house:** Higgsfield has no general music or sound-effects model available to us. Its only music model is restricted to its game pipeline, and the tool tells agents not to use it.
- **Narration:**
  - Voice and engine: preset voice id `6705e465-7b52-5915-a1d8-b1222885e01d` ("Fraser"), through Higgsfield's `text2speech_v2` model with the ElevenLabs engine.
  - Lines: R02, R03, R08, R09, R13 (5 generations).
  - Rights: commercial use is governed by your Higgsfield plan's terms. Pro plans normally include commercial rights, but **confirm in your Higgsfield account terms** before running paid ads with these.
- **Verification:** every narration was transcribed back with Whisper and checked word by word against the script.
