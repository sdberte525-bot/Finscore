## 2. What the market is telling us
1. **Demand is real and growing.** Barna (2025) found weekly Bible reading among US Gen Z rose from 30% to 49% in a year, with young men leading. Faith apparel is moving from "cheesy" to "cultured."
2. **The two failure modes are clear:**
   - Christian merch looks corny: clip-art crosses, puns, pastel script.
   - Dark gym brands look occult.

   Nobody owns **dark and unmistakably Christian** at a premium streetwear standard.
3. **Platforms reward the things you can do without a face:**
   - Instagram's top signals are watch time, likes and sends, with sends weighted more for reaching new people.
   - Since April 2026 Instagram has stopped recommending unoriginal content.
   - Mosseri's 2026 memo says polish is now "the tell."

   Original design breakdowns, real phone footage and macro detail all fit.
4. **Hashtags are capped at 5 on Instagram and don't add reach.** TikTok reads on-screen text and captions for search. That means naming content for real searches: "Christian gym shirt", "Philippians 4:13 long sleeve", "pump cover".
5. **TikTok Shop doesn't fit yet.** Its dispatch metrics (1–3 day handling) conflict with China-based print-on-demand at 10–14 days. Use TikTok for discovery and send traffic to Shopify.

## 4. Brand voice rules (all channels)
- **Short, declarative, calm.** Strength through restraint. Never "🔥🔥 INSANE DROP."
- **Say scripture plainly, cite it correctly, and don't preach.** One verse per post, no sermons.
- **Never fake anything:** reviews, scarcity, fabric claims, customer photos or AI people.
- **Recurring lines:**
  - BUILT IN SILENCE.
  - FAITH. DISCIPLINE. IRON.
  - THE WORK SPEAKS.
  - QUIET ON THE FRONT. SCRIPTURE ON THE BACK.
  - DARK AESTHETIC. CLEAR GOSPEL.
- **Banned phrases:**
  - unlock your potential
  - unleash the beast
  - level up
  - transform your lifestyle
  - elevate your performance
  - "Jesus is my spotter"-style puns

## 5. Ideas I'd push back on
- **"Not just gym clothing"** is a weak line. Every apparel brand says some version of it. Show the meaning instead: the verse breakdowns do this better.
- **Drop-culture urgency right now is dishonest.** You have two always-available items. Save scarcity for real Chapter windows, which only make sense once the list is big enough for them to matter (roughly 500+ subscribers).
