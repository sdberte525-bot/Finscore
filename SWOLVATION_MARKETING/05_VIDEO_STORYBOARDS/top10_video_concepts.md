## The top 5 to prioritise (ranked by expected customer value per hour)
1. **V3 NOT CHURCH MERCH.** It's the clearest statement of the brand's reason to exist, and it targets the exact frustration of the buying audience.
2. **V4 3 DETAILS.** It turns the artwork into the content. Built for saves and watch-through, it shows the real design.
3. **V8 THE ARRIVAL.** Real proof that the product exists, with an honest verdict. It's the strongest trust asset you can make, and it costs only the sample.
4. **V5 WHY SKULLS.** It turns the most likely objection ("too dark") into a reason to send it to a friend.
5. **V9 THE WORK SPEAKS.** The only one that needs new spend. It adds a second cinematic signature piece once organic data shows the cinematic style earns sends, not just views.

**On cinematic versus native:** the polished films (V1, V2) are your brand's "signature." But Instagram has said publicly that polish is becoming a tell, and TikTok rewards native formats. **Expect the native edits (V3–V7) and the real phone footage (V8, V10) to reach more people,** and use the films for pinned posts, the website and retargeting. Measure this rather than assume it (see `12_ANALYTICS_AND_TRACKING`).
