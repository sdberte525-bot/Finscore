# TikTok: 10 video ideas

## Rules for TikTok
- **Native beats polished.** Use text-led edits, fast pacing and real product photos.
- **Name every video for a real search.** TikTok reads on-screen text and captions. Put the search phrase in both: "Christian gym shirt", "Philippians 4:13 shirt", "oversized gym tee", "pump cover".
- **Business accounts must use the Commercial Music Library.** All five finished edits carry original sound design. You can post them as they are, or add a Commercial Music Library track in-app at low volume.
- **No TikTok Shop for now.** Its dispatch rules don't fit 10–14-day print-on-demand. Send traffic to the Shopify link in bio.
- **Post 1 a day if you can, minimum 4 a week.** Reuse every TikTok as an Instagram Reel and YouTube Short, re-exported without the watermark.

**Status:** five of the ten (T1–T5) are already produced and exported. See `13_FINAL_ASSETS/tiktok/`.

12. **Assets:** `SW_tiktok_NOT-CHURCH-MERCH_9x16.mp4`.

14. **Why it works:** it names a frustration the audience already feels, then answers it in seconds. The hook is a relatable opinion, which drives comments ("finally") and stitches.

### T7. "Unboxing the First Shirt I Ever Printed". **[SAMPLE]**
1. **Audience:** small-business supporters, plus potential buyers wanting proof.
2. **Objective:** proof the product is real, and trust.
3. **Hook:** "the first SWOLVATION shirt just arrived. let's see if it's actually good."
4. **Execution:** phone, top-down, hands only (optional):
   1. the package (address covered);
   2. pulling out the shirt;
   3. a slow reveal of the back;
   4. a macro of the real ink;
   5. a ruler on the chest width against the size chart;
   6. honest verdict text ("print: 9/10, black is deeper than our photos" or whatever is actually true).
   - **If something is disappointing, say so and say how you're fixing it.** That builds more trust than perfection.
5. **Visual style:** raw phone, natural light plus the clamp light.
6. **Length:** 15–25s.
7. **Sound:** real unboxing sound.
8. **On-screen text:** honest verdict lines.
9. **Caption:**
   > first SWOLVATION shirt off the press. honest review of our own product, no filter. printed to order, took [X] days to arrive.
10. **Hashtags:** #unboxing #smallbusiness #christianstreetwear #gymwear #swolvation
11. **CTA:** "would you wear it?"
12. **Assets:** [SAMPLE].
13. **Difficulty:** 2.
14. **Why it works:** real unboxings are among the most trusted apparel formats, and honesty about your own product is rare and memorable.
