# Instagram: 10 Reels, 5 carousels, 5 community and promo concepts

## Rules for everything on this page
- **Hashtags:** at most 5 per post. Instagram caps them at 5, and they help search, not reach.
- **Optimise for sends and saves,** not likes. Those are the signals Instagram says reach non-followers.
- **Original content only.** Instagram stopped recommending reposted or unoriginal accounts in April 2026.
- **No faces, no AI people, no fake claims.** Hands are optional and only if you're comfortable; they must be real.
- **Link:** "Link in bio" points to a UTM-tagged URL (see `12_ANALYTICS_AND_TRACKING/utm_guide.md`).

**Asset key:**
- **[READY]** means it exists today. See `13_FINAL_ASSETS` for links.
- **[SAMPLE]** needs the physical sample.
- **[FREE]** can be made with existing footage at $0.

**Difficulty** runs from 1 (under 15 minutes) to 5 (a half-day).

4. **Execution:** use the finished ad *A. Built in Silence* (15s), or its 6s cut, as the Reel.

## Community and promotional concepts

### P1. "Verse + Iron" weekly email (and an Instagram broadcast channel once eligible)
1. **Audience:** subscribers and engaged followers.
2. **Objective:** an owned audience that keeps coming back, not just discounts.
3. **Hook:** subject line "Verse + Iron · Week [n]."
4. **Execution:**
   - Every Monday, one short email: a verse, one old-school training note (a classic principle, e.g. progressive overload), and a design sketch or behind-the-scenes look.
   - Mirror it in an Instagram broadcast channel once your account qualifies.
5. **Visual style:** plain, mostly text, one image.
6. **Length:** a 90-second read.
7. **Sound:** none.
8. **On-screen text:** none.
9. **Caption (Instagram post announcing it):**
   > Every Monday: one verse, one old-school training note, one first look. Free, in your inbox. Link in bio to join. (New subscribers get 10% off their first order.)
10. **Hashtags:** #swolvation #faithandfitness #christianlifter #bibleverse #oldschoolbodybuilding
11. **CTA:** Join the list.
12. **Assets:** [FREE] (text plus existing images).
13. **Difficulty:** 2 per week.
14. **Why it works:** it gives people a reason to subscribe beyond the 10%, keeps the brand top of mind, and costs nothing at under 10,000 emails a month.

### P2. "Choose the Next Verse" (community-designed Chapter II)
1. **Audience:** followers and subscribers.
2. **Objective:** real participation, and pre-validating the next design before you make it.
3. **Hook:** a Story poll: "Next verse: Isaiah 40:31 or 2 Timothy 4:7?"
4. **Execution:**
   - Collect verses from R10's comments and email replies.
   - Shortlist 2–3 and vote via Story polls plus email reply-to-vote.
   - Reveal the winning concept sketch, then launch it as "Chapter II."
   - Only claim "you chose this" if the vote really decided it.
5. **Visual style:** design sketches; real process content.
6. **Length:** 2–3 weeks of light posting.
7. **Sound:** n/a.
8. **On-screen text:** poll stickers.
9. **Caption (reveal):**
   > You chose it. Chapter II carries 2 Timothy 4:7: "I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith." First look for the list next week.
10. **Hashtags:** #swolvation #chapterii #christianstreetwear #faithandfitness #gymwear
11. **CTA:** Vote, then join the list for first access.
12. **Assets:** [FREE] polls, and later the new artwork.
13. **Difficulty:** 2.
14. **Why it works:** co-creation builds ownership. People buy what they helped choose, and you learn demand before spending on a new design.

### P5. Independent and Christian gym seeding (month 2+)
1. **Audience:** small powerlifting and church-affiliated gyms (1K–10K followers).
2. **Objective:** physical presence where the buyers train, plus a local creator network.
