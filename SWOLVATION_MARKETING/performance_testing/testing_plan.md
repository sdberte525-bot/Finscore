# Creative testing plan

## What to track (per post, at 48 hours and 7 days)

| Metric | Where | Why it matters | Rough read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **3-second hold rate** (3s views ÷ impressions) | TikTok and IG insights | Did the hook stop the scroll? | The most important number for comparing hooks |
| **Average watch time** and **% watched fully** | TikTok, IG, YT | Did the body keep attention? | Compare between versions of the same length |
| **Rewatches / loops** | TikTok | Short teasers should loop | Teasers only |
| **Shares and saves** | All | Real interest; drives organic reach | Saves = "I want this" |
| **Profile visits** | IG, TikTok | Curiosity about the brand | Should rise as recognition builds |
| **Link clicks / CTR** | Bio link, Stories, ads | Intent | Once the site is the destination |
| **Newsletter signups** | Site | Pre-launch demand | The main pre-launch goal |
| **Add-to-cart, purchases** | Shopify/Stripe | Revenue | Only once checkout is live |

Don't judge a post on likes alone, and don't judge it before roughly 48 hours or about 1,000 impressions.

## Reading results

- A hook is a **winner** if its 3s hold rate beats the others by a clear margin across at least two posts, not one lucky one.
- High hold but low watch time means the hook works but the body doesn't. Keep the opening and re-cut the rest.
- Low hold but high completion among the people who stayed means a strong body. Test new openings on it.
- High saves and shares means scale it: a small paid boost and more variants in that concept.
