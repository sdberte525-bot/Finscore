If you offer free shipping, subtract about $5.80 from every column.

**Break-even customer acquisition cost on the first order ≈ the contribution: roughly $17–33.**
- Apparel benchmarks put Meta's cost per purchase around $24, though those figures are vendor-reported and low-confidence.
- So **paid ads are at best break-even on a first order, and likely loss-making while the account and pixel are cold.**
- Paid only becomes rational once you have:
  - organically proven creatives;
  - a working email flow that turns a share of non-buyers into buyers later;
  - ideally a higher order value, from people buying both pieces.

### Discount discipline
- **If you add a compare-at sale price later,** Shopify applies a discount code **on top of the sale price** (Shopify Help).
  - Example: a $49.99 tee "on sale" from $59.99, plus 10% off, sells for $44.99.
  - Restrict the welcome code to non-sale items, or run a sale **or** a code, not both.
- **Compare-at prices must be genuine former prices.** Inventing a "was $79" is deceptive pricing.
- **Recommended welcome code setup:**
  - Amount off products, 10%.
  - Applies to all products.
  - **Eligibility: the "Email subscribers" customer segment, one use per customer.**
  - **Does not combine** with other product discounts.
  - Combines with shipping discounts.

  Codes are per-customer and per-segment, so a code leaked to a coupon site can't be used by non-subscribers.

## 2. Scenario A: $0 (start here regardless)
**Do first:**
1. Fix the website's trust gaps (`11_WEBSITE_RECOMMENDATIONS`): the "confirmed before launch" placeholders, the 404 shipping/returns/FAQ/size pages, policies, and the welcome offer in the popup.
2. Set up the free Shopify Messaging welcome series, abandoned checkout and order emails.
3. Post 4–5 times a week on TikTok and Instagram using the finished assets (`13_FINAL_ASSETS`).
4. Build relationships with creators: comments only, no gifts.
5. Contribute in communities: r/GYM, r/weightroom, Christian lifting Discords. Contribute, don't advertise.

**Avoid:**
- Buying followers or using engagement pods.
- Giveaways that require "tag 3 friends." They attract prize-hunters, not buyers.
- Posting the same polished ad every day.

**What you'll learn (weeks 1–4):**
- Which hook and format earns sends, saves and profile visits.
- Your profile → link click rate.
- Your site → email signup rate.
- Whether anyone adds to cart.

**Risks:** slow. Expect weeks of low numbers, and that's normal.

**Success thresholds by day 30. These are my targets for a healthy start, not promises:**
- at least 1 piece of content over 5× your median views;
- 50+ email subscribers;
- a site email signup rate of 3% or more;
- at least 1 organic sale.

**When not to spend:** until the website fixes are live and the welcome flow works.

## 3. Scenario B: $60 (recommended launch)
**My allocation:**

It does four things: (1) checks print quality and the black versus the campaign images before strangers pay; (2) gives you **real faceless content for months**: flat lays, hanging, on a bench, macro of the actual ink, the real unboxing; (3) tells you the true delivery time to state on the site; (4) makes creator gifting safe later. I chose the tee over the long sleeve because it's cheaper and shows the jumbo back print, your most distinctive and riskiest feature. |
| **Custom domain** (e.g. swolvation.com, if available) | ~$12–20 / year | A `.myshopify.com` link in your bio and at checkout looks less trustworthy. Your own domain also lets Shopify Messaging send from an authenticated address, which helps keep emails out of spam. |

With your phone, this recreates the brand's single-hard-light look on the real garment, and makes real photos look like the campaign images. If money is tight, use any desk lamp you already own ($0). |
| **Total** | **~$50–65** | Anything left over goes toward a second sample or a creator gift once someone replies warmly. |

**Why not ads with this $60?** It buys roughly 40–120 clicks and 0–3 sales, far below the ~50 events a week Meta needs to optimise. That's noise, not information.

**Why not gifting with this $60?** You'd gift an unseen product to one creator, which is risky and hard to learn from.

**Do first:** order the sample on day 1. It takes about 10–14 days, so the clock starts now. Run Scenario A while it ships.

**Avoid:** posting "real" content before the sample arrives. Until then, use the campaign assets.

**What you'll learn:**
- The real quality and the real shipping time.
- Whether real phone content on the actual shirt outperforms the AI-generated campaign visuals. Instagram's 2026 direction suggests it might.

**Success thresholds:**
- **Sample passes:** print sharp, no cracking after 3 washes inside-out, black acceptable versus the photos.
- **Real-product posts beat campaign posts** on sends or saves per reach.

**When not to spend:** if the sample fails quality. Then stop marketing, fix the product or provider first, and ask PODpartner for a reprint or refund.

## 4. Scenario C: $200 (reinvest if B is positive)
**Preconditions:** the sample passed, website fixes are live, the welcome flow is working, and organic content is producing at least some link clicks.

| Item | Est. cost | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long-sleeve sample | ~$30–40 | Content for the second product, plus a fit check on the slim long sleeve |
| 3 creator gifts (first wave, US-based, warmed-up relationships) | ~$90–110 | The cheapest way to put the product in front of a qualified Christian-lifter audience with honest third-party voices |
| **Option 1: no ads.** Keep ~$50–60 for 1–2 more gifts once the first posts land | ~$50–60 | Better if gifting produces posts |
| **Option 2: a small paid boost.** $5/day for 8 days behind your best **organically proven** Reel, optimising for profile visits, landing-page views or email signups, **not purchases** | $40 | Only if a post has already out-performed (5× median sends or saves) |

**What you'll learn:**
- Creator response and post rate.
- Code clicks and orders.
- Cost per landing-page view and email signup from a proven creative.

**Risks:**
- Creators may not post: there's no obligation, and that's the honest model.
- Shipping delays.
- A boost may buy cheap views from low-intent audiences. Watch the email signup rate, not views.

**Success thresholds:**
- At least 2 of 3 gifted creators post, and at least 1 code order.
- If boosted: cost per email signup under $2, or cost per landing-page view under $0.40. These are my planning thresholds.

**When not to spend:**
- No gifting if the sample failed.
- No boosts until a Reel has organically outperformed.
- **No conversion-objective campaign until you can fund at least $20/day for 2 weeks.**

## 5. When paid ads make sense (later)
Use Meta Reels first, broad audience, Sales objective, at $20–30/day, as in `SWOLVATION_AD_CAMPAIGN/09_PAID_AD_TEST_PLAN`. Only start when all of these are true:
1. The real product is verified.
2. At least 3 organic posts have beaten your median by 3–5× on sends or saves.
3. The site converts some organic traffic: at least 1% add-to-cart from sessions.
4. You know your real contribution per order, and can afford to lose about $300 while learning.
