## My honest call: gifting is premature this week. Relationships are not.
- **Don't gift until you've held a real shirt.** Gifting a print-on-demand shirt you haven't seen risks a creator posting "the print cracked" or "it's not the colour in the photos" to their audience. One bad first impression from the only creator who mentions you costs more than the shirt. **Order one sample first** (see `10_ADVERTISING_BUDGETS`).
- **Each gift costs real money:** about $25–35 product cost plus PODpartner shipping. It arrives in **about 10–14 days**, from China. That means:
  - plan 2–3 weeks between "yes" and a possible post;
  - send to US-based creators only at first;
  - never promise fast delivery.
- **Start relationships now, at $0:**
  - Follow the 15 tier A/B creators from your brand account.
  - Leave genuine, specific comments on 3 posts a day: not "🔥", but a line about their actual lift, verse or point.
  - Share one of their posts to your Story each week, with credit.

  After 2 weeks, your DM is from a name they recognise, which roughly triples the odds of a reply. That's my experience-based estimate, not a measured figure.
- **Affiliate works best after gifting:** a code is worth nothing to a creator until they own and like the shirt.

## How the collaboration works
| Option | What they get | What you ask | Cost | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Relationship | Genuine engagement, shares | Nothing | $0 | Now |
| Gift, no obligation | One shirt in their size | Nothing required. If they post, they must disclose it as gifted | About $30–40 each | After the sample is approved |
| Gift + affiliate | Shirt plus their own code: 10% off for followers, **15% commission** to them | Optional post with disclosure | Gift plus 15% of attributed sales | Same time as the gift, as an offer |
| Paid | A fee per post | A specific deliverable | $50+ | **Not yet.** Only after a creator's code shows sales |

**Margin check:** a 10% follower discount plus 15% commission on a $49.99 tee costs about $12 on each sale. That's affordable only if your PODpartner cost is under about $28 (see unit economics). If it's higher, offer 10% commission.

## Required disclosures (FTC, US)
- **A free product is a "material connection."** Any post must disclose it, even if you didn't ask them to post.
  - Acceptable wording: **"Gifted by @swolvation"**, or **"#ad"** if there's payment or commission.
  - "#freeproduct" on its own isn't recommended.
- **Affiliate links or codes** need a clear statement near the link, for example: "I earn a commission if you use my code."
  - In video: say it or put it on screen, **and** write it in the caption.
- **Your duties as the brand:** give creators the disclosure line, check their posts, and ask them to fix a missing disclosure.
- **Never ask for a positive review, and never script opinions.** Ask only for honesty.

Source: FTC Endorsement Guides FAQ, cited in `01_BRAND_AND_COMPETITOR_RESEARCH/market_research_full.md`.

## The three outreach messages
Personalise the bracketed parts every time. If you can't write a specific line about their content, they're not the right creator.

### 1. Friendly introduction (relationship, no ask). Send after about 2 weeks of genuine engagement.
> Hey [first name], your [specific video, e.g. "deadlift PR where you talked about Joshua 1:9 before the pull"] stuck with me. We run a small gym label called SWOLVATION. It's gothic, old-school, and every piece carries a verse on the back. No ask here, I just wanted to say your content is the kind of faith-and-iron we're trying to make clothes for. Keep going.

### 2. Product gifting offer. Only to someone who replied warmly, or after a few real exchanges.
> Hey [first name], thanks for the reply earlier. I'd like to send you a piece, no strings. Either the Joshua 1:9 tee (boxy, big back print) or the Philippians 4:13 long sleeve (thorn vines down both sleeves). You don't have to post anything. If you do want to share it someday, just mark it "gifted by @swolvation" so it's honest with your followers.
>
> One heads-up so there are no surprises: we print each shirt to order, so it takes about 2 weeks to arrive. If you're in, send me your size (the chart is here: [size guide link]) and an address you're comfortable with. If not, no worries at all.

### 3. Affiliate or commission proposal. Only after they've received the shirt and reacted positively.
> Glad the [tee / long sleeve] landed well. If you ever want to share it, I can set you up with your own code: [CODE] gives your followers 10% off, and you'd get 15% of every order that uses it, paid monthly. No quotas and no posting schedule. Use it only if you actually like wearing it. If you post, please include "#ad – I earn a commission from my code" so it's above board. Want me to set it up?

**Follow-up rule:**
- **One** gentle follow-up 5–7 days after an unanswered message: "No pressure. Just floating this back up in case it got buried."
- Then stop. Never send a third message.

## Sample economics (print-on-demand reality)
- Gifts are ordered through PODpartner as normal orders to the creator's address. Either:
  - place a manual order in PODpartner, or
  - create a Shopify order with a 100% discount so fulfilment flows automatically.

  You pay PODpartner's product and shipping cost either way.
- **Budget:** 5 gifts ≈ $150–200. That's Scenario C money, not Scenario B (see budgets).
