# SWOLVATION: Creator / Influencer Prospect List

Research date: **2026-10-11**. No one has been contacted.

**How the numbers were checked.** Every follower count marked "verified" was read from the creator's own public profile on 2026-10-11, using one of these sources:
- **TikTok:** the `followerCount` / `heartCount` fields embedded in the public page `tiktok.com/@handle`.
- **Instagram:** the public web-profile JSON behind `instagram.com/handle`. This also gave the median likes and comments on the 12 most recent posts.
- **YouTube:** the subscriber and video counts in the channel page header, plus the view counts of the 6 newest uploads from the `/videos` tab.

Where a platform blocked the request or returned nothing, the entry says **not verified**. I invented no handles. Every creator below came from a search result, a third-party list (IQFluence public pages, a podcast page) or a YouTube search results page, and the profile was then opened directly. I did not collect contact emails, although some appear in public bios.

**Cost key**
- **$0 relationship:** follow, engage, share their content, and maybe send a handwritten note. No product.
- **~$30–40 gift:** one POD shirt (~$25–35) plus shipping.
- **Affiliate %:** a unique code at 10–20% commission, usually paired with the gift.
- **Paid later:** only after the creator has shown sales through their affiliate code.

## Discovery process (repeatable, for the founder)

### Qualifying criteria (all should be true before gifting)
1. **Content fit:** Faith signal (scripture, "Christ is King", ✝️/☦️ in bio, faith talk in videos) **and** lifting. Or a strong old-school / dark-vintage lifting aesthetic. Or gymwear/streetwear review content.
2. **Size:** 1k–100k followers. Nano (1k–10k) creators usually convert best per dollar and reply more often.
3. **Active:** Posted within the last 14 days. Several candidates above look dormant (FaithAndPlates, Alex Goose, Tali).
4. **Engagement health:**
   - Instagram: median likes on the last 12 posts of at least ~1% of followers, with real comments.
   - TikTok: views on recent videos in line with follower count. A total-likes-to-followers ratio of roughly 10x or more is typical of healthy accounts.
   - YouTube: recent videos average ~5–20% of subscriber count.
5. **Audience location:** Mostly US (or your shipping region). IQFluence's free pages show country splits. Some "Christian gym" accounts on that list had audiences that were 75–90% Nigeria/Zimbabwe/Zambia.
6. **No conflicts:** Not running their own Christian apparel brand, not tied to an exclusive apparel ambassadorship (e.g., Darc Sport), and no content that clashes with the brand (explicit content, open PED promotion).

### Search steps
1. **TikTok:** Search `#christianfitness`, `#christiangym`, `#faithandfitness`, `#christianbodybuilder`, `#jesuslifts`, `#oldschoolbodybuilding`, `#goldenerabodybuilding`, `#pumpcover`. Sort by recent and open profiles with 1k–100k followers.
2. **Instagram:** Use the same hashtags plus a bio-keyword search ("Christ is King" + coach, "Philippians 4:13", "natural bodybuilder"). Check "Suggested for you" on a good fit (e.g., @fit.stann) to find similar accounts.
3. **YouTube:** Search "Christian bodybuilder", "bodybuilding as a Christian", "natural pro card vlog", "OCB pro card", "WNBF prep", "pump cover review", "Christian clothing haul", "Christian streetwear brands", "Darc Sport haul", "golden era training". Filter by upload date (this month).
4. **Competitor tagging:** Look at who tags or reviews Saint Kaizen, Iron Therapy Club, Darc Sport, Elevated Faith, HWLF and similar brands. Their reviewers already buy this category.
5. **Free aggregator pages:** IQFluence public "top-influencers" pages (e.g., `christian-gym-influencers`, `christian-fitness-influencers-instagram`, `natural-bodybuilder-influencers`) list handles, follower counts, ER and audience country. Always re-check on the live profile.
6. **Podcasts:** Search Apple/Spotify/Buzzsprout for "faith fitness", "Christian men fitness", "1 Timothy 4:8". Small shows (Fit For The Kingdom, God Gains & Grit, Temple Keepers, The 4:8 Men Podcast) are open to guests and sponsor swaps.
7. **Local / underground gyms:** Use Google Maps for "powerlifting gym" / "hardcore gym" / "barbell club" near you, then check each gym's Instagram (aim for 1k–10k followers with member-photo posts). Faith-based gyms (e.g., Holy Spirit Gym in Costa Mesa, Holy Grit Fitness, Faith Rx'd chapters) came up in research. I could not confirm their Instagram handles, so check them manually.
8. **Track everything** in a sheet: handle, platform, followers, date checked, median views/likes, last post date, fit score, status (watch / engaged / gifted / posted / code sales).

### Red flags
- **Fake followers:** Follower count far above engagement (e.g., 120K followers and ~100 likes per post). Sudden follower spikes. Many followers with no profile photo or no posts. Audience concentrated in countries the creator doesn't live in or speak to.
- **Engagement pods:** The same small group of accounts leaving generic comments ("🔥🔥", "Great post!", "Amazing bro") within minutes of every post. High likes but no real conversation.
- **Giveaway-farmed audiences:** A feed full of "follow + tag 3 friends to win" posts. Followers who joined for prizes rarely buy. Expect engagement to collapse between giveaways.
- **Engagement-rate anomalies:** Third-party ER above 100% (IQFluence showed 547% and 4,348% for two listed accounts) means a single viral post or bad data. Look at the median, not the average.
- **Brand conflicts:** Creator owns a Christian apparel line (Stephen Covert / Iron Therapy Club; possibly Showtime Marv / Divine Visions; Christ_glorifiedfitness / Three Nails). Or is an exclusive ambassador for a competing gymwear brand.
- **Content conflicts:** Explicit or sexualised content, open PED/steroid promotion (judgment call: "enhanced" honesty is not the same as promotion), or hostile or divisive posting that would embarrass a faith brand.
- **Dormancy:** No posts in 30+ days. A gift will probably never get posted.

### Outreach etiquette (for later; nothing was sent)
Engage genuinely for 1–2 weeks before reaching out. Offer the gift with **no posting obligation** and mention the affiliate code as optional. Follow FTC disclosure rules (#gifted / #ad) on any post.
