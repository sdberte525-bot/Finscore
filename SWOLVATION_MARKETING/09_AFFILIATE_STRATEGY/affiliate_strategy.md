# Affiliate strategy

## My call
**Set it up when your first creator agrees to a code, not before.** An affiliate program with no affiliates is admin with no return. It costs $0 to have ready, so prepare the settings now and switch it on in about week 3–4.

## Tool choice (pricing checked 11 Oct 2026; confirm on the App Store before installing)
| Option | Cost | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shopify Collabs** | Free; 2.9% fee on each commission payout | It isn't accepting new creator sign-ups right now, so it's unreliable for recruiting new nano creators |
| **GoAffPro** | Free tier: unlimited affiliates and sales, basic portal | **Recommended first.** $0 and enough for 5–20 creators |
| **UpPromote** | Free plan (reported caps on approvals and revenue); Growth $29.99/mo + 2% | A good second choice; upgrade only if volume justifies it |
| Refersion | From $29–39/mo + 3% | Too expensive at this stage |
| **Manual codes** (Shopify discount codes per creator) | $0 | Works for the first 3–5 creators. Track orders by code in Shopify Analytics → Discounts, and pay commission by PayPal monthly |

**Start manually with Shopify discount codes for creators 1–5, then move to GoAffPro once there are more than 5.**

## Terms
- **Follower discount:** 10% off with their code (e.g. `CROSIER10`). Use one code per creator, so attribution is clean.
- **Commission:**
  - 15% of the net product subtotal, after discounts and excluding shipping and tax.
  - Paid monthly by PayPal once over $25.
  - Withheld on refunded orders.
- **Combination settings:**
  - Creator codes **don't combine** with the welcome 10% code or any sale.
  - They do combine with free-shipping offers if you run any.
- **Cookie or attribution window** (if you use GoAffPro links): 30 days.
- **Disclosure** required on every post (see the influencer doc). It's in the affiliate agreement.
- **One-page agreement:**
  - Commission rate and payment schedule.
  - Disclosure requirement.
  - No paid search on the brand name.
  - No coupon-site posting.
  - Either side can end it anytime.

## Margin guardrail
Commission plus the follower discount on a $49.99 tee costs about $12. Run the numbers in the unit-economics sheet (`12_ANALYTICS_AND_TRACKING/swolvation_dashboard.xlsx` → "Unit economics") with your real PODpartner cost:
- **Tee contribution after those costs below $8:** drop commission to 10%.
- **Above $15:** you can offer 20% to top performers.

## A customer referral program (later)
Once you have **25 or more customers**, add "give 10%, get 10%" referrals through GoAffPro's customer referral feature, or UpPromote's.

Before that, real customers are too few to refer anyone.

## Community "Iron Brotherhood" ambassadors (month 2–3)
- Invite customers who post the shirt themselves (with permission) to become ambassadors:
  - their own code;
  - a first look at new designs;
  - a vote on the next verse.
- It's cheaper than influencers and more believable.
- Real customers only, never invented.
