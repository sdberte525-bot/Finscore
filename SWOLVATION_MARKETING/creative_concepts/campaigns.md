# Three campaigns

All three follow the rules in `brand_strategy/brand_direction.md`: no people, real product only, typeset brand text, and sound-led edits. They share one identity but hit different emotions.

---

## A. THE UNDERGROUND

**Emotion:** Tension, then respect. **Objective:** Make the brand feel serious and earned, not merchandise. **Audience:** Lifters who train early and alone; people tired of loud fitness brands. **Platforms:** TikTok, Reels, Shorts, and the 15s/30s YouTube cut. **CTA:** THE FIRST DROP.

**World:** An old basement gym before dawn. One caged lamp. Concrete, rubber, scratched steel. Chalk in the knurling. A bar still settling after someone dropped it. The room shows the work; whoever did it is already gone.

**Visual direction:**
- Low angles, slow motion-control pushes, macro texture.
- Hard top light, deep black.
- Cool shadows with warm highlights, fine grain.

**Sound direction:**
- Room tone, the lamp's hum, chalk ticking on rubber, a single plate rattle.
- One deep sub hit on the reveal.
- A full beat of near-silence just before it.
- Music is minimal: low drone and pulse, never a "motivational" track.

**Hooks (test these):**
1. Sound-first: a loaded bar crashes into frame. Black. "NOT EVERYONE WILL UNDERSTAND."
2. "5:02 AM." over the empty lit platform. (Native, quiet, curious.)
3. Macro knurling in chalk. "NO CROWD."
4. "THE GYM IS EMPTY. THE WORK ISN'T DONE."
5. "BUILT FOR THE WORK." over the shirt hanging on the rack.

## B. THE VINTAGE ERA

**Emotion:** Nostalgia and reverence. **Objective:** Tie SWOLVATION to the golden age of bodybuilding, as heritage rather than costume. **Audience:** Fans of old-school bodybuilding and vintage gym wear, aged 18–35. **Platforms:** Reels and Instagram feed (strongest for saves and shares), Shorts. **CTA:** MEET SWOLVATION.

**World:** A '90s gym in late-afternoon sun. Chrome dumbbells, a wooden locker bench, painted brick, an analog wall clock, dust in the window light. The shirt hangs on a wooden peg like it has always been there.

**Visual direction:**
- 35mm film emulation: golden tones, halation, slight gate weave, lifted blacks.
- Slow pans and static frames, like a lost reel.

**Sound direction:**
- Projector clatter or a cassette deck click.
- Distant clank of iron and a ticking clock.
- A warm, slightly lo-fi instrumental with tape hiss.

**Hooks:**
1. "THE CULTURE NEVER LEFT."
2. "SOME THINGS DON'T NEED UPDATING."
3. Film burn into the sunlit locker room, then silence, then a clock tick.
4. "FOUND IN THE BACK OF THE GYM." (Playful, native to TikTok.)
5. "IRON SHARPENS IRON."

## C. THE NEW GENERATION

**Emotion:** Desire. **Objective:** Make the garments look covetable as fashion pieces. **Audience:** Younger gym-goers who buy on looks; Instagram and TikTok fashion browsers. **Platforms:** Reels, TikTok, feed stills, the website. **CTA:** THE FIRST DROP.

**World:** A black studio. The shirt floats on an invisible form and turns 360°. Macro light sweeps across the cracked print. Clean flat lays on rubber with iron and chalk.

**Visual direction:**
- Precise and symmetrical.
- Fast but deliberate cuts on the beat.
- Deep blacks with crisp cream.

**Sound direction:**
- Modern minimal electronic: tight kick, sub bass.
- Fabric whooshes on cuts, a hard stop before the end card.

**Hooks:**
1. "THIS ISN'T JUST ANOTHER GYM SHIRT."
2. "NOT MADE TO BLEND IN."
3. Macro of the cracked "Be strong and courageous." arch with no text, a 1.5s hold, then a cut to the full turn.
4. "TURN AROUND." (The 360 reveals the back.)
5. "FRONT: QUIET. BACK: LOUD."

---

## Which one leads

**The Underground leads the launch** (hero film, teasers). It's the most distinctive without people, and its sound design works muted or loud.

**The New Generation** carries the product ads, because the shirt is the subject and the 360 and studio footage are the most accurate product views.

**The Vintage Era** is the shareable feed and Reels story for week 2, when the audience already knows the name.
