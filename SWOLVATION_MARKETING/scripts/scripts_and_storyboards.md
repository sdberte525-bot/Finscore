# Scripts and storyboards

Times are in seconds on the finished timeline. Shot IDs (`v44`, `t31`, …) match the exact prompts and Higgsfield job IDs in `storyboards/shot_list.md`.

There are no people anywhere, and every word on screen is typeset in the edit. The music is an original synth score built for these ads (see "Sound" in `production_manifest.md`).

## 1. Hero film: "THE IRON WAITS" (Underground), 25.9s, 9:16

**Objective:** launch statement. **Audience:** lifters 18–35. **Platforms:** Reels, Shorts, TikTok (pinned), website hero. **CTA:** THE FIRST DROP.

**Idea:** an empty basement gym before dawn. Everything in it shows someone has been working: a dropped bar, chalk, worn iron. Then the uniform is revealed.
