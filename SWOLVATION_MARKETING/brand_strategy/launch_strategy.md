# Launch strategy

No claims of guaranteed reach or sales. This is a plan that can be measured and improved.

## A. Launching with no following

The problem is that nobody has seen the brand yet, so the first job is recognition, not sales.

1. **Look established before asking for anything.** Before the first ad runs, put 9 posts on the Instagram grid and 6–9 videos on TikTok, so a new visitor lands on a profile that looks like a real brand. The finished assets cover this (see the manifest).
2. **Pre-launch, not "buy now".** Checkout isn't live yet, so every CTA is THE FIRST DROP, COMING SOON or MEET SWOLVATION. Collect interest with the website's newsletter form, once `NEWSLETTER_WEBHOOK_URL` is set. A list of people who asked to be told is worth more on launch day than cold traffic.
3. **Organic first, then put a small budget behind proof.** Post organically for 1–2 weeks and watch which video holds attention best. Then put a small, fixed daily spend behind that winner only (Meta Reels and TikTok Spark Ads boost an existing post). Don't spend on videos nobody watches organically.
4. **Show up where lifters already are.** Reply to and engage genuinely on posts about old-school bodybuilding, home gyms and faith-and-fitness. Use the brand account, and never post fake customer comments.
5. **Real people later, honestly.** Once shirts physically exist, the most credible content is real: real lifters (friends, local gyms) wearing them, filmed simply. Mark gifted product as gifted. That content will outperform any AI footage for trust. The AI footage builds the world and the AI-free footage proves it's real.

## B. Content strategy by platform

| Platform | What to post | Cadence (first 30 days) | Lead assets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Instagram Reels** | Cinematic brand films, product reveals, the Vintage Era story | 4–5 per week | Hero film, Vintage Era, Not Made To Blend In, product ads |
| **Instagram feed** | 4:5 product photos, close-ups of the prints, scripture | 3 per week | `product_photography/`: gym-hook, flat-lay and studio shots |
| **Instagram Stories** | Countdown sticker to the drop, polls ("tee or long sleeve?"), the newsletter link | Daily during launch week | Stills plus teasers |
| **TikTok** | Native-feeling posts first, cinematic cuts second, sound-led hooks | 5–7 per week | 5:02 AM, Sound On teaser, product ads, hook variants |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Same verticals as Reels, with the longest hero cut | 3 per week | Hero film (25.9s), product ads |
| **YouTube in-stream** | 16:9 15s only once there's a budget | Paid only | THE IRON WAITS 15s |

**Content pillars** (rotate so the feed doesn't feel like nonstop ads):
1. **The world.** Empty gyms, iron, chalk, light (Underground and Vintage).
2. **The product.** The 360 turns, print close-ups, front vs back.
3. **The meaning.** The verse behind each shirt (Joshua 1:9, Philippians 4:13), stated plainly, not preached.
4. **The drop.** Countdown, "the first drop", launch day.
5. **Real people (later).** Real lifters in the real shirts, once available.

**Captions:** one line, no hashtag walls. 3–5 relevant tags (#oldschoolbodybuilding #gymwear #faithandfitness #powerlifting #vintagegym). Add the scripture reference when a shirt is featured.

## E. Optimization loop (weekly)

1. Every post gets a hook ID (H1–H6, see `scripts/scripts_and_storyboards.md`) and a concept ID (Underground, Vintage, New Gen). Track them in `performance_testing/testing_plan.md`.
2. **Week 1–2:** post a different hook each day on the same body edit. After 48 hours, compare 3-second hold rate and average watch time.
3. **Keep the top 2 hooks, drop the bottom half.** Make new variations of the winners: same opening, a different second shot, or a different line.
4. **Paid (week 3+):** start a small fixed budget on the single best organic post. Judge it after it has had enough impressions to mean something, not after a few hours.
5. Once checkout is live, switch the CTA to the shop and judge by click-through rate, then add-to-cart, then purchase.
6. **Production follows data.** If the Vintage concept wins, the next credits go to more Vintage shots, not a fourth concept.
