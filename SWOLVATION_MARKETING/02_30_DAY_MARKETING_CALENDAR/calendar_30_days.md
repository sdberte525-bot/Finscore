# 30-day launch calendar

**Before day 1 (day 0, about 3 hours):** finish the website fixes in `11_WEBSITE_RECOMMENDATIONS` (placeholders, pages, policies, popup offer) and switch on the welcome email series. **Order the sample on day 0.** It takes about 10–14 days, so real-product content starts around day 16.

**Realistic load:** one main post a day, cross-posted to TikTok and Instagram, plus 2–3 Story days a week. That comes to about 20–40 minutes most days and about 60 on shoot days. If a day slips, skip it. Don't double up.

**Posting windows:** these are starting points to test, not rules. After 2 weeks, use your own Instagram and TikTok analytics ("most active times") and move posts there.

**The mix over 30 days:**
- 8 product videos
- 6 meaning/story posts
- 4 Iron Verse series posts
- 4 real-product proof posts (after the sample)
- 4 community prompts
- 2 trust/education posts
- 2 recaps or reviews

Promotion is limited to the always-on "10% for subscribers" in 4 Story reminders. **No sales countdowns.**
