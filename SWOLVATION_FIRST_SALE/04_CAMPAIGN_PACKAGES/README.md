# Phase 4: The first 5 campaign packages

**What's in each package:**
- a 9:16 video in 3 hook variants (A, B and C), H.264 1080×1920 with AAC audio, loudness normalized;
- a 1080×1920 Reel cover;
- an Instagram caption and a TikTok caption;
- 3–5 hashtags;
- a CTA and a test plan.

**How they were made:**
- Every video is built from **real approved artwork**: verified AI product shots of the actual prints, checked frame by frame in earlier rounds.
- They contain **no people, hands or mannequins.**
- The sound is original, made in our pipeline (no licensed songs).
- **0 Higgsfield credits** were used for this phase.
- **Nothing has been posted.**

**Files:**
- **Videos:** download links in the table below. They're hosted in your Higgsfield library; the ZIP has everything.
- **Covers:** download links in the table below, plus the ZIP.
- **Reproduce or re-cut:** the sandbox edit lists are `~/mk/edl/fs_*.json`. The generator is `~/mk/fs.py`, mirrored in the repo at `SWOLVATION_FIRST_SALE/04_CAMPAIGN_PACKAGES/fs.py`.

**About the covers:**
- Title text sits between y 1300 and 1620. That's inside the 3:4 crop Instagram's profile grid shows, and below the print, so no scripture is covered.
- **On Instagram:** upload as the Reel cover, then "Adjust profile image" and keep it centered.
- **On TikTok:** choose the cover in the editor, or use frame 0. Hook A's first frame works as a fallback.

**Captions:**
- They avoid fabric claims, scarcity and invented reviews.
- "Printed to order" is true because the shirts are print-on-demand.
- Prices are the live prices on 11 Oct 2026.

## Short product ads
P1, P4 and P5 run 7–11.4s and already work as short product ads. For a 6s cut, trim P5 hook A to 0–7.6s: hook, both backs, front, tee price. That's a one-line EDL change.

**No paid ads have been set up.** If you later approve a $5/day test, use P5 hook A with the "Shop now" button to `/collections/all`. Stop when it reaches a $15 cost per add-to-cart, or 7 days with no add-to-cart.

## How to judge the hook test (after 48 hours, each variant at least 300 views)
1. **3-second hold rate:** TikTok Studio retention graph, or Instagram "Skip rate" on Reels insights. This is the main metric.
2. **Average watch time:** tiebreaker.
3. **Profile visits per 1K views:** shows buying interest.
4. Post the winning hook to your main grid and use it on the next re-cut. **Change one thing at a time.**
