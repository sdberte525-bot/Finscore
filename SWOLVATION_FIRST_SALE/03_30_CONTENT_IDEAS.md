# Phase 3: 30 content concepts, ranked

**Rules for every concept:**
- No people, hands, faces, bodies, mannequins or human shadows.
- No physical shirts are filmed; we don't own samples yet.
- No founder on camera.
- Only the real approved artwork. No claims about fabric weight, print method or durability.
- No popular songs. Use original gym sound (made in our pipeline), the TikTok Commercial Music Library, or Meta Sound Collection. See `02_TREND_RESEARCH.md` §3.

**Footage key:**
- `g1–g17`, `t30/t31`: existing verified AI clips in the Higgsfield library and sandbox.
- `n1/n2/n7/n8fix/n15clean/n16`, `p_teeflat/p_lsflat`: verified stills.
- "New" means a new generation is needed: still = 2 credits, 5s video = 8.75 credits. **Nothing new has been generated. Every new generation needs your approval and a balance check.**

**Cost/time:**
- 0 cr means it's built from existing footage in our edit pipeline (about 20–40 minutes each).
- Hashtags: 3–5 per post. Instagram caps them at 5, and they help discovery, not reach.
