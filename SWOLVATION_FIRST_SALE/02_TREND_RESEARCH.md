# SWOLVATION Research Phase 2: Organic Reach, Content, and Conversion

Research date: 2026-10-11. Labels: **CONFIRMED** = stated by the platform or backed by data. **HYPOTHESIS** = creator/marketer opinion or our own inference. Each item lists source URL and publish/updated date (or "date not shown").

## Top 5 takeaways
1. **Small accounts can get reach. Followers are not the gate.** TikTok says follower count is not a direct For You factor. Instagram tests Reels with small pools of non-followers first. For non-follower reach, *sends* (DM shares) weigh most, alongside watch time and likes (CONFIRMED, §1).
2. **Original footage only.** Since April 2026, Instagram excludes mostly unoriginal accounts from all recommendation surfaces (CONFIRMED, §1). Shoot your own product footage. Use Trial Reels to test hooks on non-followers for free.
3. **No popular songs in brand posts.** Use the TikTok Commercial Music Library, Meta Sound Collection, or original gym-sound audio. Bang Energy lost to the major labels over TikTok promo videos (CONFIRMED, §3).
4. **Hashtags are labels, not reach.** Instagram caps them at 5 and says they do not increase reach (CONFIRMED, §5).
5. **The quickest way to get people wearing the shirt on camera, without paying, is affiliates.** TikTok Shop Open/Target Collaboration lets creators earn commission (CONFIRMED, §8), plus campus/Faith RX'd group codes (HYPOTHESIS, §9). Fit and size content tackles the #1 apparel return reason (CONFIRMED, §8).


**Method note.** Direct page fetching was blocked in this environment (proxy denied about.instagram.com, newsroom.tiktok.com, techcrunch.com and others), so official statements were confirmed through search-engine extracts of the official pages and reputable trade press quoting them. Where only a secondary source was available, that is stated. Re-open the official URLs before quoting them publicly.

## 1. Formats that get reach for low-follower accounts (TikTok + Instagram Reels)

**Instagram**
- **CONFIRMED: Top 3 ranking signals are watch time, likes and sends (DM shares).** Mosseri told creators to watch *average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach*. Likes matter slightly more for content shown to followers; **sends matter slightly more for content shown to non-followers**, which is the audience a near-zero-follower brand depends on. Comments were not named. Source: Social Media Today, "Instagram shares algorithm insights" (Jan 2025), https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/
- **CONFIRMED: Instagram's ranking deliberately tests small accounts.** April 2024 change: Reels are first shown to a small pool likely to be interested, then to bigger batches if they engage. Mosseri: "help small creators reach a broad audience that might be much larger than the number of people who actually follow their account". The same update replaced reposts with originals in recommendations and removed aggregators. Source: TechCrunch, 2024-04-30, https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/30/instagram-is-updating-its-ranking-systems-to-surface-more-content-from-smaller-original-creators
- **CONFIRMED: Originality enforcement expanded to all formats (photos, carousels, Reels).** Accounts that mainly post unoriginal content are no longer eligible for recommendation surfaces (Explore, Reels, suggested posts). "Original" means you filmed/created it, or meaningfully transformed third-party material. Source: PetaPixel, 2026-04-30, https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/ ; MediaPost, https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/414773/instagram-algorithm-update-deprioritizes-unorigina.html (2026, exact date not shown). *Implication (HYPOTHESIS):* SWOLVATION must shoot its own footage. Do not repost golden-era bodybuilding clips or other creators' edits as main content. Stock or AI clips should be heavily edited with original text.
- **CONFIRMED: Trial Reels** (available to all professional accounts since Dec 2024) are shown **only to non-followers** first. After 24 hours you see views/likes/comments/shares and can publish to the grid, or auto-publish based on performance in the first 72 hours. Source: TechCrunch, 2024-12-10, https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/10/instagram-rolls-out-trial-reels-that-arent-shown-to-a-creators-followers ; Engadget, Dec 2024, https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-will-let-video-creators-make-trial-reels-for-non-followers-160016725.html. *Implication (HYPOTHESIS):* use Trial Reels to A/B test hooks (e.g. 3 versions of the same Joshua 1:9 reveal). With few followers, it costs nothing.
- **CONFIRMED: Reels can be up to 3 minutes** (Jan 2025). Mosseri said there is no single "perfect" length; the goal is retention. Source: Social Media Today, Jan 2025, https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-officially-expands-reels-length-3-minutes/737766/
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Keep Reels under 90 s for recommendation reach (Buffer guidance, not an Instagram statement). Source: Buffer, "Instagram Reels length" (date not shown), https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reels-length
- **CONFIRMED (data): carousels have the highest engagement rate on Instagram** (0.50% Q2 2026, vs Reels 0.48%, images 0.33%). Reels engagement has fallen two quarters in a row. Source: Socialinsider "2026 Instagram Organic Benchmarks" (Q2 2026 data), https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-benchmarks/. *Implication (HYPOTHESIS):* use Reels for reach and carousels (detail shots, verse breakdown, size chart) for engagement and saves.

**TikTok**
- **CONFIRMED: For You signals** are (1) user interactions (likes, shares, follows, comments, content created), (2) video information (captions, sounds, hashtags), (3) device/account settings (weighted least). **Follower count and previous high-performing videos are not direct factors.** Completing a longer video is a strong signal. Source: TikTok Newsroom, "How TikTok recommends videos #ForYou", 2020-06-18, https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/ ; updated explainer 2023-11-01, https://newsroom.tiktok.com/how-tik-tok-recommends-video-for-you?lang=en-MY
- **CONFIRMED (data): posting frequency.** Buffer analysed 11.4M TikTok posts from 150K+ accounts. Compared with posting once a week, 2–5 posts/week gave about 17% more views per post, 6–10 gave about 29% more, and 11+ gave about 34% more. Median views per post stayed around 500. Posting more mostly increases the odds that one video breaks out. Source: Buffer, 2025-10-08, https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/
- **HYPOTHESIS: Formats to prioritise:** 7–15 s loops (easy completion), carousels/photo mode, and series ("Drop 001 part 2"). This follows from completion being a strong signal. It is our inference, not a TikTok statement.

## 2. Hook patterns for product-only (faceless) apparel videos

- **CONFIRMED: Front-load the product.** TikTok: "over 63% of the videos with the highest click-through rate" present their key message or product in the **first 3 seconds**. Source: TikTok for Business, "9 creative tips to drive auction ad performance" (ad research, date not shown in extract), https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/9-creative-tips-to-drive-auction-ad-performance. *Caveat:* this comes from ads data. Applying it to organic posts is our inference.
- **CONFIRMED: TikTok's apparel creative tips** suggest putting price/promo in the first 3 seconds, showing a range of products, and ending with a CTA plus logo. TikTok itself notes that new accounts may want to hold off on the hard-sell pattern. Source: TikTok Creative Center, "Creative tips: Fashion & Apparel" (date not shown), https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/quicktok/online/creative-tips-fashion-apparel/pc/en
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Faceless ads have less personality to rely on, so they work best with an early proposition, large text overlays (many people watch with sound off) and close-up texture or motion as the opening frame. Source: vendor blogs Zeely, "Faceless video ads examples" (date not shown), https://zeely.ai/blog/faceless-video-ads-examples/ ; Snaklab Creative Center guide (date not shown), https://snaklab.lovable.app/en/blog/tiktok-creative-center-guide/
- **HYPOTHESIS: Hook bank for SWOLVATION.** These are our inference from the principles above. Each puts the print or verse on screen by about 1 s.
  1. **Back-print flip:** the tee hangs front-side out. A hand (no face) spins it, the Joshua 1:9 back print fills the frame, and text reads "the back is the point."
  2. **Text-question hook:** "Which verse do you lift to?" over a slow push-in on the print. Built for comments (see §6).
  3. **Contrarian:** "Christian shirts don't have to look like youth-group merch." Then cut to the skull/thorn graphic. This plays on the existing debate (§6).
  4. **Scripture-as-cue:** black screen, then the words "Be strong and courageous." Then hard cut to a barbell drop and the shirt. The verse becomes the voiceover or caption.
  5. **Pattern interrupt:** the shirt draped on a loaded barbell, plate on top, chalk puff, then reveal. An object in an unexpected context is a common vendor pattern. Source: Sovran, "10 TikTok ad hook ideas to test in 2026" (date not shown), https://sovran.ai/blog/tik-tok-ad-hook-ideas
  6. **Number/list:** "3 details you missed on this tee" (macro shots of the print, neck tag, boxy cut). Also drives saves.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Avoid opening on a slow "beauty shot." Open on movement or the print. TikTok's apparel guidance favours a fit reveal or a quick change over a slow product pan, according to the secondary summary at Mega Digital, "TikTok ad creative" (2026), https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/tiktok-ad-creative/

## 3. Trending sounds and commercial rights

- **CONFIRMED: On TikTok, business accounts must use the Commercial Music Library (CML).** TikTok says CML tracks are pre-cleared for commercial use (more than 600K tracks). General-library songs are **not** cleared for brand content, and using them to promote a business risks infringement. Source: TikTok for Business, "Explore royalty-free music in our Commercial Music Library" (date not shown), https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/audio-library-royalty-free-music
- **CONFIRMED: TikTok restricted business-account music in 2020.** Business accounts were moved to the commercial catalog only. Source: Social Media Today (2020), https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-changes-rules-on-music-usage-by-businesses/577734/ ; Business Insider (May 2020), https://www.businessinsider.nl/tiktok-music-restrictions-brands-royalty-free-commercial-use-copyright-infringement-2020-5
- **HYPOTHESIS (vendor):** The CML licence covers TikTok only. Reposting the same video with that track to Instagram or YouTube needs a separate licence. Source: Soundstripe, "TikTok Music Library explained" (Aug 2026 per search extract), https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/tiktok-music-library-explained
- **CONFIRMED: Instagram restricts the licensed music library for some business accounts.** Accounts without access can use **Meta Sound Collection** (14,000+ royalty-free songs and sounds, safe for Reels and Stories). Source: Instagram Help Center (date not shown), https://help.instagram.com/ipad-app/402084904469945 ; Meta extended Sound Collection to Reels ads (Oct 2022): Music Business Worldwide, https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/meta-offers-royalty-free-music-library-to-instagram-reels-advertisers
- **HYPOTHESIS (vendor):** Meta describes its licensed library as for personal, non-commercial use. Even tracks that *appear* available can lead to claims or removal when used promotionally. Switching to a Creator account to unlock songs does not change who holds the commercial rights. Source: Sonilo (date not shown), https://sonilo.com/ai-music/royalty-free-music-for-instagram-reels
- **CONFIRMED: The legal risk is real, for brands of any size.** Bang Energy was sued by all three major labels over more than 100 TikTok promo videos. The court ruled that Bang infringed Universal's music in about 140 videos (Bloomberg Law, 2022-07-12, https://news.bloomberglaw.com/ip-law/bang-infringed-music-in-scores-of-tiktok-promo-vids-court-says) and partially granted Sony's motion (2022-09-14, MBW, https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/sony-music-scores-partial-victory-in-copyright-infringement-lawsuit-against-bang-energy). Bang's argument that the TikTok licence covered it was rejected for liability purposes. Overview: Bloomberg Law, 2022-12-06, https://news.bloomberglaw.com/ip-law/tiktok-music-lawsuits-fire-warning-shots-to-brands-influencers. Sony also sued Marriott over about 1,000 recordings used in social posts, including posts by paid influencers. Source: Reed Smith (date not shown), https://www.reedsmith.com/articles/viral-and-deadly-when-music-goes-bad-on-social/
- **HYPOTHESIS: SWOLVATION rules.**
  1. Use a TikTok Business account with CML sounds only. On Instagram, use Sound Collection or original audio.
  2. Build **original audio**: barbell clank, chain rattle, heavy breathing, plate drop, a whispered verse. It is free, cannot be claimed, matches the dark aesthetic, and others can reuse it, which spreads the brand.
  3. Ride trends through **format** (the edit structure, text template), not the copyrighted song.
  4. Gifted creators post from personal accounts, but the post is still brand promotion. Brief them to use CML or Sound Collection audio.

- **HYPOTHESIS: Organic playbook ($0–low cost).** These are our inference, so test them.
  1. **Find local units:** search Instagram for "[university] FCA", "[university] Chi Alpha / XA", "[university] Cru", "Faith RXD [city]". Most campus chapters run their own Instagram for weekly meetings. Follow them and comment genuinely on their posts first.
  2. **"Verse of the week" assets:** offer free, unbranded or lightly branded verse graphics for chapters' Stories. Credit links back to SWOLVATION. Shareable assets are the lowest-friction ask.
  3. **Huddle / small-group discount code:** a group code (e.g. FCA-[school]) with 10–15% off, or a "buy 5 get 1" for a Huddle. Avoid "official FCA" language. FCA, Cru and Chi Alpha are trademarked organisations, so never imply endorsement.
  4. **Fundraiser model:** offer chapters a share per shirt sold through their code ($3–5) for mission trips. Churches and campus groups regularly sell shirts to raise funds. POD means no inventory risk.
  5. **Faith RX'd / Christian CrossFit and barbell clubs:** offer a free tee as a raffle prize at a chapter workout and ask for a group photo. This becomes the brand's first "people" content via UGC. Find clubs with the Google Maps "barbell club" / "powerlifting gym" method in research_creators.md.
  6. **Church youth / young-adult pastors:** DM a short, non-salesy note offering 1 free shirt for a youth leader who lifts. Young adult ministries are where the 46% weekly-attending Gen Z men are (§6).
  7. **Content collabs with ministry pages:** Instagram Collab posts (shared to both accounts) with a chapter's "workout + devotional" Reel. The verse and the brand appear in their audience's feed.
  8. **Respect the setting:** keep pitches about encouragement and community. Never sell during worship or ministry events without permission.
