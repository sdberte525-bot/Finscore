# Phase 5: 7-day first-sale sprint (Mon 12 Oct to Sun 18 Oct 2026)

**Goals:** 100 new followers (TikTok and Instagram combined), 500 site visits, 10 email subscribers, 1 sale.

These are **goals, not guarantees.** Buffer's analysis of 11.4M TikTok posts found a **median of about 500 views per post** (research §1). A brand-new account can miss all four goals in week 1 and still be on track. The dashboard's decision rules say what to change.

**Nothing in this plan has been posted, sent or paid for.** Every post, DM, email and purchase is yours to approve and do.
