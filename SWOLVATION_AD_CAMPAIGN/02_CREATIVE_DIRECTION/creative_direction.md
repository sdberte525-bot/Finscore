# Creative direction: paid social, first drop

## The idea
**The garment is the relic. The gym is the sanctuary. The work happens in silence.**

Competitors sell bodies, discounts and countdowns (see `01_BRAND_RESEARCH`). SWOLVATION sells two shirts with scripture on the back. The ads behave accordingly:
- quiet
- product-first
- shot like objects in a worn, sacred place

## Absolute rules (from the brief, enforced in every frame)
- **No people:** no models, faces, bodies, hands, silhouettes, human shadows, mannequins or testimonials.
  - One generated clip grew a wrist out of the sleeve at 4.0s and was cut at 3.2s. See `05_GENERATED_SHOTS`.
- **The real artwork only:**
  - Every garment frame was generated from the real product photos as references and checked against the original artwork files (`SWOLVATION_PRINT_FILES/source/`).
  - Where a generated print didn't match, the real print file was composited in instead of regenerating (the tee-front still).
  - Camera moves are slow and single, so the print can't drift.
- **No unverified claims:** nothing about fabric weight, print method, durability, comfort or fit beyond what the size guide states.
- **No scarcity, no dates:** "Drop 001" means the first release. No countdowns, "limited", "selling out" or "last chance".
- **No VFX clichés:** no explosions, flames, lightning, glowing eyes, spinning 3D logos, fake flares, heavy smoke or whip-pans.
- **No licensed music:** all sound is original and synthesised for these ads (room tone, iron impacts, plate rings, ticks). There is no risk of a copyright claim.
- **No banned phrases:**
  - Unlock your potential
  - Unleash the beast
  - Level up your workout
  - Transform your lifestyle
  - Elevate your performance

## Look
| | |
| --- | --- |
| Light | One hard source (caged bulb, a light blade, a raking side light). Deep falloff. |
| Places | Underground gym, raw concrete, rubber floor, iron, chalk. Product studio on matte charcoal concrete. |
| Grade | Warm-neutral: rich blacks, cream highlights, slightly desaturated. Campaign B is hard black and white. |
| Texture | Light, even film grain on every shot so stills and generated video sit together. |
| Camera | One slow move per shot: overhead push, slider, macro glide, locked-off light change. Never handheld chaos. |

## Typography
- **Headlines:** Big Shoulders 800, uppercase, tight.
- **Labels and CTA:** Archivo 500, uppercase, widely tracked.
- **Logo:** the brand mark is the real cross and SWOLVATION wordmark from the print file, never retyped.
- **Colour:** bone `#F5F3EF` on black, with the cream logo as printed.
- **Safe area (9:16, 1080×1920):** text sits between y 290 and 1250 and x 170 and 910. That keeps it clear of the Reels/TikTok top bar, the caption and CTA block, and the right-hand buttons.

## Sound
- **Bed:** room tone (low brown noise, faint 50 Hz hum, air), which is the "silence".
- **Accents:**
  - weighted sub impacts on hard cuts
  - an inharmonic iron-plate clank
  - long low metallic rings under titles
  - dry mechanical ticks on product cuts
  - a filtered air swell before reveals
- **No voiceover.**
- **Mastering:** mixed to −14 LUFS integrated, −1.5 dBTP.

## Is "Drop 001" accurate?
Yes. These two shirts are the brand's first release: the site's collection is "The first drop" (`/collections/new-drop`), and nothing came before it. The ads say "Drop 001" and nothing else:
- no release date
- no quantity
- no "limited"

If a second release happens, the next is "Drop 002".

## Known gap to fix in production
The campaign imagery shows a washed, vintage-black garment, because the reference photos were of a washed sample. PODpartner prints on a standard black blank, which can look deeper and less faded in person.
- Before scaling spend, photograph one real PODpartner order and compare.
- If the colour differs noticeably, either regrade the ads slightly deeper or replace the overhead shots with real photos.

This is listed in `10_QUALITY_REVIEW` as the main product-accuracy risk.
