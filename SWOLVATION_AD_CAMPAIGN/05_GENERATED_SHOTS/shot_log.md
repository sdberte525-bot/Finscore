- **Paid retakes:** only one, n16. It was a deliberate fix: the prompt was rewritten to stop a duplicate-garment artefact.
- **Every other defect** was fixed by trimming, cropping, repainting the backdrop or compositing the real artwork. No credits went to those.
- **References:** every garment generation used the real product photos plus a previously verified generation: tee back `fe70e5bd`, long-sleeve back `26c1ba30`, tee front/back `dc3c2fc7`, tee chest `b2fcf71a`, and the verified frames listed in `03_APPROVED_PRODUCT_REFERENCES`.
- **Prompts** spell out every word of each print, say "same size and position", and end with a negative block: no people, hands, mannequins, added text, extra logos, misspellings, warping or CGI look.
- **Presets:** Higgsfield's "IN THE DARK" preset was declined each time it was suggested, and the literal prompt was generated.
- **Full prompts** are stored with each job in your Higgsfield library.
