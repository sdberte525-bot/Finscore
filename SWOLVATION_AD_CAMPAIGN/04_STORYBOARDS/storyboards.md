# Storyboards, shot plans and editing strategy

**Format for every ad:**
- 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 High, AAC 48 kHz.
- −14 LUFS integrated (±0.4), no clipping.

**Text:** inside the safe area (y 290–1250, x 170–910). The Reels/TikTok UI covers the top 290 px, the bottom 670 px and the right 170 px.

**Shot IDs:**
- `g*` and `n*` are new in this campaign (see `05_GENERATED_SHOTS`).
- `t*`, `v*`, `k*` and `p_*` are approved launch footage (`SWOLVATION_MARKETING/storyboards/shot_list.md`).

Timecodes are those of the rendered files.

**One rule shapes every edit:** the garment is on screen in the first frame, and words never sit on top of the print.
