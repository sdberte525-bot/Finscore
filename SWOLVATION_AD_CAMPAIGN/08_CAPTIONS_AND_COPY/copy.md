# Captions and ad copy

**Voice:** short, declarative, sure of itself. The scripture is on the shirt, so the copy doesn't preach.

**Claims rule:** nothing here makes a claim about fabric weight, print method, durability or comfort, and there is no urgency or scarcity language. Prices are the current Shopify prices: tee $49.99, long sleeve $59.99. Update them here if they change.

**Banned phrases (none appear below):**
- unlock your potential
- unleash the beast
- level up your workout
- transform your lifestyle
- elevate your performance

**Link:** use the product or collection URL with UTM tags, for example:
`?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=drop001&utm_content=C_tee`

## Words to avoid
- Fabric claims: heavyweight, premium cotton, won't fade, buttery, breathable.
- "Limited", "selling fast", "last chance", "only X left", countdowns.
- Reviews or testimonials until real customers write them.
