- full-resolution crops of every print in every new shot (first, middle and last frame of each clip)
- ffprobe for format
- EBU R128 for loudness
- the safe-zone overlay for text

## What holds up
- **Prints:**
  - Every visible line of scripture is spelled correctly: "Be strong and courageous.", "JOSHUA 1:9", "I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.", "PHILIPPIANS 4:13".
  - The SWOLVATION wordmark is correct wherever it appears.
  - Skulls, crosses and sleeve vines match the artwork files.
- **Front graphics:** the tee front in C uses the real print file (n8fix → g13). The long-sleeve front (k28, v48) matches the slim thorned-cross artwork.
- **No people:**
  - No skin, hands, faces, silhouettes or mannequins appear in any final.
  - Two generated tails that drifted toward a human shape (g4's wrist, v44's background shape) were cut out entirely.
- **No banned material:** no flames, lightning, lens flares, spinning logos or excessive smoke. No banned phrases. No invented claims or scarcity.
- **Sound:** original sound design, mastered to −14 LUFS, consistent across all seven.
- **Type:** brand fonts throughout, inside safe areas, never over the print.

## Shortcomings (honest)
1. **Garment colour vs the real product (the biggest risk).**
   - Every image shows a washed, vintage black.
   - Your PODpartner blank is standard black and may look deeper and flatter.
   - Until a real order is photographed, the ads may over-promise a vintage wash.
   - **Action:** compare with the first real shirt. If it differs, regrade the ads slightly deeper or reshoot the overhead shots with the real shirt. Don't scale spend before this check.
2. **AI realism.**
   - At normal viewing the shots read as photographed.
   - Close inspection shows slightly too-perfect fabric in the studio turns (v10, v48) and an idealised drape on the overhead flat lays.
   - A sharp-eyed viewer may suspect CGI on the turns. That is the main reason realism is scored 7.5–8, not 9.
3. **The tee's studio-front turn (v10, C-tee 5.8–8.6s).**
   - Its chest emblem renders the thorn ring as two side sprigs, close to the real artwork but not exact.
   - It is on screen at small size, mid-turn, for under 3s.
   - **To improve:** replace it with a real photographed front/back turn once samples arrive.
4. **Hooks:**
   - A opens dark and slow by design (your sequence). Good for brand, weaker as a cold paid hook. Use A for organic and retargeting, not as the first cold ad.
   - D opens on 0.8s of logo-on-black before the first product frame. If D's hook rate is weak, test a version that starts on the off-white tee.
5. **Sound design.**
   - It is synthesised, so it is clean and copyright-free.
   - It isn't as rich as a professional Foley session, and real recorded fabric and iron would add realism.
6. **Overhead stills:** the sleeve tips touch the frame edge because the garments fill the frame. The graphics are complete; it's a cropping choice.
7. **n15clean (off-white long sleeve):** the backdrop above the collar was repainted to remove a ghost artefact, and the collar's top edge is very slightly soft at full size.
