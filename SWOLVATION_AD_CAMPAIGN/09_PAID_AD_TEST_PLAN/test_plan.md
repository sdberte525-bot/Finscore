# Paid social test plan: first 4 weeks

**Approval required:** nothing has been published, no billing is connected and no spend is authorised. Everything below is a recommendation. You approve before anything goes live.

**Assumptions:**
- A new brand with no pixel history and no customer list yet.
- Two products: tee $49.99 and long sleeve $59.99.
- The budget figures are a suggestion. Scale them to what you're comfortable losing while you learn.

## 0. Before spending (one-time setup)
1. **Meta Business Manager:**
   - Connect the Instagram account and the Facebook page.
   - In Shopify, install the **Facebook & Instagram** sales channel. It sets up the Meta pixel and Conversions API.
   - Verify that PageView, ViewContent, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout and Purchase fire. Use a test order, then refund it.
2. **Shopify store:**
   - Finish the steps already listed: rename the products, archive the old ones, create the six pages, set a shipping rate.
   - Place one real PODpartner test order.
3. **Product check:** photograph the real shirt when it arrives.
   - If the black or the print differs from the ads, fix the ads first. See `10_QUALITY_REVIEW`.
4. **UTM tags** on every ad link, as in `08_CAPTIONS_AND_COPY`.
5. **TikTok:**
   - Create a TikTok Business account.
   - Install the TikTok channel in Shopify (TikTok pixel).
   - Spark Ads need organic posts first.

## 2. Paid phase 1, weeks 1–2: find the creative that stops the scroll
**Campaign:** Meta Sales objective (Advantage+ shopping, or a manual Sales campaign optimised for **Purchase**).
- If purchases are too rare for the system to learn (fewer than about 10 a week), switch the optimisation event to **AddToCart** for these two weeks. Return to Purchase once volume allows.

**Placements:** Advantage+ placements, with Reels and Stories carrying most of the spend. All files are 9:16 with safe-zone text.

**Audience:**
- Broad: US, ages 18–40, all genders, no interests.
- New-brand creative tests on Meta usually learn faster broad than on narrow interest stacks, and the creative itself does the targeting.
- Optional second ad set with loose interests: bodybuilding, weight training, Christian music or ministries, streetwear. Compare the two.

**Budget:** suggested $20–30 a day in total.

**Ads in the test (one ad set, Advantage+ creative off so the edits stay as made):**

| Ad | Creative | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | C-tee: THE UNIFORM (11s) | Conversion, tee |
| 2 | C-long sleeve: THE UNIFORM (11s) | Conversion, long sleeve |
| 3 | D: DROP 001 (8.6s) | Launch, both pieces |
| 4 | B6: FAITH. DISCIPLINE. IRON. (6.6s) | Identity hook |
| 5 | Still: QUIET ON THE FRONT (4:5) | Static control |

Hold A (15s brand film) for phase 2 retargeting and organic use. It is the slowest burn.

**What to watch, and rough thresholds to act on.** These are directional benchmarks, not promises.
- **Hook rate** (3-second views ÷ impressions): above about 25% is healthy for Reels. Below 15% means the opening frame isn't working.
- **Hold rate** (ThruPlays ÷ 3-second views).
- **Link CTR (outbound):** above about 1% is healthy. Below 0.5% is a weak ad.
- **Cost per landing-page view** and **cost per add to cart.**
- **Purchases and cost per purchase.** With a $50–60 product, a cost per purchase below about $25–35 suggests the unit economics can work. Check this against your real margin after the PODpartner base cost and shipping.

**Rules:**
- Leave each ad about 3–4 days, or at least about 1,000 impressions, before judging it.
- Turn off an ad that has spent about 2× your target cost per purchase with no add to cart.
- Don't edit live ads; duplicate them. Edits reset learning.

## 3. Paid phase 2, weeks 3–4: scale what worked, start retargeting
1. **Winners:** move the top one or two ads into a new campaign and raise the budget by about 20–30% every 2–3 days while cost per purchase holds.
2. **Retargeting ad set** (about 20% of budget):
   - **Audience:** video viewers at 50%+ of any ad, Instagram engagers, site visitors and add-to-carts in the last 30 days, excluding purchasers.
   - **Creatives:** A (BUILT IN SILENCE), C for the product the person viewed, and the stills.
3. **Next creative tests:**
   - **Background:** black concrete vs off-white. The off-white overheads exist (g16, g17), so make C variants with them.
   - **Hook variant:** C-tee opening on the chalk-settling overhead (g11) vs the plain overhead.
   - **Copy:** scripture-led vs "Quiet on the front. Scripture on the back."

## 4. TikTok (organic first, then a small paid test)
- Post the same videos organically for 2 weeks. TikTok favours native posts.
- Then run a **Spark Ads** test on the two best-performing organic posts:
  - $20 a day, 7 days
  - objective: website conversions, or traffic if the pixel lacks data
  - broad audience
- TikTok's audience skews younger. Watch the add-to-cart rate, not only views.

## 5. When to spend more
Increase the budget only when **all** of these hold for at least 5–7 days:
- cost per purchase at or below your break-even (price minus PODpartner cost, shipping, fees)
- outbound CTR stable or rising
- frequency below about 2.5 in prospecting
- the real product has been checked and returns or complaints show no accuracy problem

If none of the creatives produce add-to-carts after about $300–400 in total spend, stop and fix the offer or landing page before making more ads. Check the product page, shipping cost at checkout and size guide.

## 6. Reporting (weekly, 15 minutes)
A one-page table: spend, impressions, hook rate, CTR, cost per landing-page view, add to carts, purchases, cost per purchase, and ROAS per ad. Note one decision per week.

## Not doing
- No discount codes or countdowns in ads, which goes against the brand's positioning on urgency. If a launch discount is wanted later, say so plainly and truthfully.
- No influencer or UGC content with people. The brand rule for ads is no people.
- No promises of results.
